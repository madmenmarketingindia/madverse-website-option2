Frequently asked questions

Before the build.
Let's get clear.

A few practical answers before we start, from choosing the setup to getting your team ready for launch.

What do you need to get started?

Share your product range, business goals, brand materials and any existing store or systems. We use the initial discussion to understand the brief and identify what the build needs to include.

How long does an ecommerce build take?

Timing depends on the design, catalogue size, integrations and readiness of your content. We agree a schedule with review points once the scope is clear.

How much will the store cost?

The proposal reflects the agreed design, features, setup and integrations. Platform subscriptions, paid apps and other third-party costs are identified separately so you can understand the ongoing commitment.

Which ecommerce platform should we use?

We review your catalogue, selling model, team workflows and integration needs before recommending a platform. We can also assess whether your current setup can support the changes you need.

Can you move our existing store?

Migration can be included after reviewing the current platform and data. We agree what can move, how existing URLs should be handled and what needs testing before the switch.

Will you upload our products and content?

Catalogue setup and content entry are agreed in the scope, including the number of products and variants. We confirm what your team will supply, the format needed and any additional copy or imagery work.

Can you connect our payment and shipping tools?

We review compatibility with the chosen platform before confirming an integration. The scope sets out the required accounts, access, configuration, testing and any third-party fees.

Who manages the store after launch?

We agree account ownership, access and handover responsibilities before launch, then show your team how to manage the store. Ongoing maintenance, support and new features can be scoped separately.
