Care at the centre.

A warm digital display system that brings a mother-and-baby moment, the product and a direct message together without visual noise.

Project overview

A caring moment.
A disciplined message.

The campaign needed to feel human before it felt promotional.

We created a compact visual language led by the mother-and-baby relationship, then anchored it with a recognisable bottle silhouette and short message architecture that could travel across display formats.

The organising thought

Connection first.
Product close behind.

Human moment→Product cue→Clear recall

The design challenge

Hold the emotion.
Lose the clutter.

01 · Emotional balance

The mother-and-baby relationship needed to remain genuine and reassuring while supporting clear brand communication.

02 · Format compression

Every unit had to retain its essential story even when reduced to the smallest display placements.

The adaptable system

One story.
Multiple proportions.

Recognition stays intact.

Warm whites, the human interaction, red-orange typography and the dark product bottle create a repeatable visual signature across the campaign family.

Designed to read

The outcome

A caring story became
a recognisable digital system.

The final campaign preserves warmth while applying the discipline needed for digital display—clear in hierarchy, consistent across sizes and unmistakably connected from one placement to the next.
