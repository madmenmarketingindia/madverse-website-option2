Dabur Hommade · Google Display Network

Convenience in.
Flavour intact.

A food-first digital display system that makes Hommade’s product range feel useful, appetising and immediately at home in the kitchen.

Project overview

Make cooking easier.
Keep food desirable.

Convenience only feels valuable when the final meal still looks worth making.

We organised the campaign around a simple visual equation: recognisable ingredients, a clear product range and an appetising end result. The system makes usefulness visible without losing the warmth of homemade food.
