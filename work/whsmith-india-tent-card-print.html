WHSmith India · Tent Card

Turn a store visit.
Into participation.

Client
WHSmith India
Campaign
Click a Shelfie
Discipline
Retail Print

Project overview

Explain the contest.
Make joining feel easy.

WHSmith India needed a tent card that could turn an in-store selfie idea into a simple, engaging customer contest.

The design had to organise participation steps and prizes without losing the playful energy of the Click a Shelfie idea.

Core
Tent Card Design
Content
Contest Mechanics
Experience
In-store Activation
Action

The visual idea

Retail attention.
Social participation.

The physical card begins the interaction inside the store, while the shelfie mechanic carries it into a shareable digital moment.

Navy creates a stable information field, while pink and yellow accents separate the contest name, instructions and reward.

The information system

One invitation.
Four simple steps.

01

Notice

A bold campaign name catches attention at the retail counter.

02

Collect

The customer asks for the Shelfie placard from store staff.

03

Click

A phone turns the in-store interaction into a personal image.

04

Upload

Sharing the picture completes participation and unlocks the prize opportunity.

The work in context

Contest instructions
that invite action.

Both supplied applications are displayed in full so the campaign title, participation steps and prize area remain completely readable.

The outcome

Retail engagement.
Made easy to enter.

The tent card turns a store visit into a guided participation journey, connecting physical retail, customer-created imagery and a clear reward.
