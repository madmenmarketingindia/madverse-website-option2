Project overview

A familiar oil.
A clearer digital reason to choose it.

A compact A+ content system that turns Dabur Badam Amla’s formula, ritual and hair benefits into one persuasive shopping journey.

The visual language pairs a lush natural environment with warm golden ingredient cues, helping the product feel nourishing, premium and immediately understandable.

Brand
Dabur
Product
Badam Amla Hair Oil
Scope
A+ Commerce Content
Outputs
Product Story · Digital Assets

The challenge

Make nourishment
visible before
the first use.

01 · The task

Explain what makes the formula useful while keeping the story quick enough for a shopper to scan.

02 · The opportunity

Use the contrast of deep green and ingredient gold to connect natural care with a richer product experience.

The content strategy

Show the ritual.
Then reveal
the reason.

Begin with an action shoppers already understand.

A four-step usage story creates familiarity first; ingredient proof and benefit communication then deepen confidence in the product.

  1. 01

    Simplify the routine

    Turn application into four clear, visually connected steps.

  2. 02

    Frame the formula

    Give Brahmi Amla extract, almond oil and Vitamin E distinct visual roles.

  3. 03

    Land on outcomes

    Close with the benefits that matter in everyday hair care.

The A+ showcase

Proof in the formula.
Payoff in the hair.

Two focused modules move from what is inside the bottle to what the shopper wants from it.

The outcome

Easy to follow.
Rich enough to remember.

A concise commerce narrative combines practical education with a recognisable, premium product world.

01

Fast comprehension

The routine reads clearly without requiring long supporting copy.

02

Visible nourishment

Ingredient frames make the formula feel tangible and purposeful.

03

Benefit-led finish

The final module leaves shoppers with distinct reasons to choose the product.

Every frame adds understanding.Together, they add confidence.
