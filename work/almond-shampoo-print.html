Dabur Almond Shampoo · Packaging Print

Nourishment first.
Offer impossible to miss.

Client
Dabur
Discipline
Print
Deliverable
Promotional Pack

Project overview

A product pack.
Designed as a festive gift.

The assignment was to create the complete printed outer pack for Dabur Almond Shampoo, bringing product, description, identities and a promotional offer into one coherent face.

The design keeps the shampoo as the hero while making the bundled Nivea Fresh Comfort offer instantly visible. Almond and milk cues support the nourishment story without competing with the purchase proposition.

Message

Copy + Logos + Pack

The packaging challenge

Two products.
One unmistakable hierarchy.

01 · Recognition

Dabur Almond Shampoo had to remain the primary purchase cue even with a second branded product visible inside the pack.

02 · Information

Offer language, product description, logos and pack details needed to remain legible without making the carton feel crowded.

The front-of-pack architecture

Recognise.
Desire. Decide.

  1. 01

    Recognise

    The Dabur mark and Almond Shampoo name establish the product immediately.

  2. 02

    Desire

    Almonds, milk and glossy hair translate nourishment into visible appetite appeal.

  3. 03

    Decide

    The festive-offer device and clear product window make the added value easy to understand.

The communication balance

Benefit leads.
The offer closes.

The current Dabur product story centres on Almond Vita Complex and milk extracts. The printed carton makes that nourishment territory familiar through ingredient imagery, then uses red promotional devices to turn product interest into a clear festive-value proposition.

The work in context

Shelf impact.
Shown at street scale.

The supplied environmental mockups test whether the promotional pack remains clear from a distance. Every visual is shown uncropped so the carton, product window and offer system stay complete.

The outcome

A complete pack.
A quicker purchase story.

The final packaging brings product recognition, nourishment cues and promotional value into a clean reading sequence—helping the bundle feel generous without becoming visually overloaded.
