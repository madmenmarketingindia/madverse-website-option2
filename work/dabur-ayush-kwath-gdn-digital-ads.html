Project overview

Grounded in routine.
Designed for recognition.

The campaign needed to feel composed before asking for attention.

We organised the visual story around a calm human moment, a strong product silhouette and familiar botanical ingredients. The result is a simple hierarchy that remains recognisable across changing digital proportions.
