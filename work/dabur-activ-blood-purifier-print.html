Dabur · Flagship Print

Seven days.
One clear promise.

Client
Dabur
Brand
Activ Blood Purifier
Discipline
Print + Outdoor

Project overview

Package the benefit.
Make it impossible to miss.

Dabur Activ Blood Purifier needed a flagship print expression that could work on the product pack and command attention on advertising hoardings.

The communication was built for women seeking clearer, healthier-looking skin, translating the product’s pimple-care proposition into one fast, memorable promise.

Primary
Flagship Print
Application
Product Packaging
Extension
Outdoor Hoardings
Audience

The communication challenge

Explain less.
Register faster.

01 · Clarity

Turn a functional wellness proposition into a simple benefit that can be understood in a passing glance.

02 · Scale

Build one composition strong enough to retain its hierarchy across a compact pack and a large outdoor format.

The organising idea

7 days.
Pimple pe vaar.

The numeral becomes the visual anchor. It gives the eye an immediate entry point, while the compact benefit line turns product performance into an easy-to-retain message.

Portrait, promise, ingredient cues and pack are arranged in one direct reading path—human first, benefit next, proof and product close behind.

The print system

Built to work
at every distance.

In the real world

One master visual.
Two outdoor moments.

The artwork maintains its message whether encountered at street level or viewed from across the road. Every supplied visual is shown at its natural ratio, without cutting the creative.

A product truth.
Made publicly memorable.

The flagship print created one recognisable visual system for packaging and advertising hoardings—keeping the benefit immediate, the product visible and the Dabur identity unmistakable.
