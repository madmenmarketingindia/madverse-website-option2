Project overview

Make home-style comfort
part of every journey.

Travel changes the view. It should not mean leaving familiar food behind.

Pack Kiya Kya? turned Haldiram's Ready-to-Eat range into a simple pre-travel ritual: before the bags close, pack the comfort of home.

The challenge

Turn convenience
into an emotional choice.

01 · Human truth

Long trips and unfamiliar places often create one shared craving: food that tastes reassuringly like home.

02 · Brand opportunity

The range needed one memorable prompt that could hold curries, rice combinations and instant meals together.

The organising idea

One useful question.
Endless travel moments.

“Pack Kiya Kya?” works as a checklist, a mnemonic and a friendly reminder—all in three conversational words.

We built the complete campaign system around that line, carrying one recognisable voice through the idea, visual world, creator scripts, production and rollout.

  1. 01

    Prompt

    Make the brand part of the moment every traveller already knows: packing the bag.

  2. 02

    Prove

    Show convenient products answering different tastes, journeys and away-from-home situations.

  3. 03

    Multiply

    Let distinct creators tell fresh stories while the campaign question keeps recall consistent.
