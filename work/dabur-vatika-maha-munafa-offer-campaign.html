Dabur Vatika · Trade Engagement Campaign

Offer ka message.
Bollywood ke andaaz mein.

Client
Dabur Vatika
Discipline
Campaigns
Channel
WhatsApp

Project overview

Trade communication.
Made worth forwarding.

The Maha Munafa Offer needed the sales network to understand—and remember—prizes, deadlines and participation details.

Instead of another instruction-heavy update, we created entertaining WhatsApp communication inspired by the language everyone already knew: Bollywood.

Audience
Sales Network
Format
Memes + GIFs
Content
Comic Strips
Objective
Offer Participation

The challenge

Explain every detail.
Feel nothing like a circular.

01 · Information density

Prizes, stock movement, deadlines and participation steps needed to be communicated without confusion.

02 · Attention in the group

The content had to compete with a constant WhatsApp stream and remain entertaining enough to share.

The organising idea

Famous dialogues.
Fresh business.

Popular Hindi-film moments were re-written around each stage of the offer. Familiarity delivered the hook; sharp copy delivered the information.

  1. 01

    Recognise

    Open on a character, scene or dialogue the audience instantly knows.

  2. 02

    Reframe

    Twist the original line around an offer update, deadline or reward.

  3. 03

    Respond

    Close with a clear action that moves participation forward.

The creative world

Seven filmi moments.
One offer story.

Each creative works alone inside a WhatsApp conversation, while the campaign codes—yellow, green, speech bubbles and offer branding—hold the series together.

The WhatsApp rhythm

One message
for every contest moment.

01

Launch

Introduce the offer, prizes and reason to participate.

02

Explain

Break mechanics and requirements into memorable creative bites.

03

Remind

Use humour to keep deadlines and stock targets visible.

04

Accelerate

Build urgency as each leg of the contest approaches its close.

Key deliverables

Built for the phone.
Designed for recall.

Memes

Single-frame humour for quick announcements and reminders.

GIF moments

Motion-led reactions that brought offer communication to life.

Comic strips

Sequential storytelling for mechanics that needed more explanation.

Campaign system

Consistent branding across every leg, update and deadline.

The outcome

Essential information.
Entertainment value included.

A trade-engagement campaign that replaced passive instruction with recognisable humour, giving the sales network a clearer and more shareable way to follow the offer.

01

Fast comprehension

One message and one action stayed central to every creative.

02

High familiarity

Bollywood references reduced the effort needed to enter each message.

03

Campaign continuity

Every update felt like the next episode of one ongoing story.

Information that landed.Humour that travelled.
