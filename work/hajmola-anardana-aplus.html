Project overview

Make digestion
feel full of flavour.

A lively A+ content journey that brings Hajmola Anardana’s taste, digestive ingredients and everyday relevance into one memorable scroll.

The system combines jewel-red pomegranate imagery, warm food moments and unmistakable Hajmola energy—keeping product education useful without losing the fun.

Brand
Hajmola
Variant
Anardana
Scope
A+ Commerce Content
Outputs
Product Story · Digital Assets

The challenge

Balance the fun.
Keep the function
credible.

01 · The task

Showcase the distinctive Anardana flavour while communicating the digestive role of its traditional ingredient blend.

02 · The opportunity

Turn a functional digestive product into a vibrant companion for the moments when people simply want to enjoy their food.

The content strategy

Lead with taste.
Support with
ingredient truth.

Use flavour to attract and familiar ingredients to reassure.

The sequence begins in the Anardana world, reveals ginger, cumin, black salt and black pepper, then takes Hajmola into real eating occasions.

  1. 01

    Own the flavour

    Make pomegranate colour, texture and abundance the immediate visual signature.

  2. 02

    Show what is inside

    Organise traditional ingredients around the pack with clear functional cues.

  3. 03

    Make it part of life

    Place the product across dining moments before opening into range discovery.

The A+ showcase

From every meal
to every mood.

The final modules move the product from functional proof into culture, occasions and the wider world of Hajmola flavours.

The outcome

Useful by nature.
Unmistakably Hajmola.

A connected commerce story makes the product’s digestive purpose clear while preserving all the flavour, humour and cultural energy of the brand.

01

Flavour first

The Anardana variant is immediately distinct and appetite-provoking.

02

Ingredient clarity

03
