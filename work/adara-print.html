Adara Beauty & Wellness · Brand Identity

Beauty, refined.
Wellness, made visible.

Client
Adara
Location
Gurgaon
Scope
Logo + Print System

Project overview

One luxury identity.
Every client touchpoint.

Adara is a beauty and wellness salon in Gurgaon offering a broad mix of hair, skin, grooming, make-up and spa experiences.

We created a brand identity that could feel composed across the complete client journey—from first introduction through the salon profile and visiting card to the moment a guest browses the service menu.

The identity challenge

Feel luxurious.
Stay warm and welcoming.

01 · Positioning

The brand needed premium polish without becoming distant, ornamental or difficult to use.

02 · Consistency

A single visual language had to organise brand storytelling, extensive service information and personal contact details.

The visual idea

A face within
a flowing form.

The mark combines a poised profile with a continuous organic silhouette. It can be read as hair, a leaf or a protective gesture—bringing beauty and wellness together without literal salon imagery.

Wide letter spacing gives the name calm authority, while a restrained gold-on-white expression adds finish without visual noise.

The identity system

Recognise once.
Remember everywhere.

  1. 01

    Profile

    Introduces the salon story, experience and range with a composed editorial hierarchy.

  2. 02

    Menu cards

    Organise a broad service offering so guests can browse and decide with ease.

  3. 03

    Visiting cards

    Carry the premium identity into a compact and personal brand exchange.

The brand language

Four rules.
One calm presence.

01

Space

Generous margins keep every format composed.

02

Contrast

White grounds the system; dark type protects readability.

03

Signature

The gold mark provides a consistent premium focal point.

04

Rhythm

Measured typography makes detailed information feel light.

The identity in context

Quiet at first glance.
Distinctive on the second.

The supplied applications focus on the master identity. They are shown without cropping so the complete symbol, wordmark and material effect remain visible.

The outcome

A salon identity.
Designed as an experience.

The completed system gives Adara one recognisable presence across brand introduction, service discovery and personal connection—premium in expression and practical in use.

01

02

03

Beauty in the detail.Calm in the whole.
