Project overview

Make invisible protection
feel visible and trustworthy.

A connected A+ journey that explains Odomos Cream through duration, clinical credibility, skin comfort and family safety.

The design uses shield forms, cool blues and warm family moments to make a technical protection promise feel human, understandable and easy to choose.

The challenge

Communicate safety.

Present a serious mosquito-protection benefit while reassuring parents about comfort, application and suitability for children.

Turn the shield into a flexible visual device that carries protection through outdoor play, indoor comfort and restful nights.

Lead with proof.
