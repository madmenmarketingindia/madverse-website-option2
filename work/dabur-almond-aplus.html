Project overview

Turn formulation science
into a reason to believe.

A benefit-led A+ journey that explains how Dabur Almond Hair Oil nourishes, strengthens and protects damaged hair.

The system balances ingredient proof with premium product imagery, using gold for nourishment, burgundy for brand recognition and black to dramatise healthy hair.

The challenge

Explain the science.
Keep the benefit
effortless.

01 · The task

Communicate a multi-ingredient protein story without making the product page feel technical or overloaded.

02 · The opportunity

Turn every formulation detail into a direct, visible hair benefit that shoppers can understand in seconds.

The content strategy

Build the proof.
Layer by
layer.

Give every ingredient a specific job in the story.

Almond, Vitamin E, onion and soya protein move from individual functional cues into one connected promise of stronger, softer and healthier-looking hair.

  1. 01

    Map ingredient to function

    Make nourishment, antioxidant protection, circulation and strength easy to scan.

  2. 02

    Visualise repair

    Use close-up strand graphics to translate technical action into visible improvement.

  3. 03

    Close with product advantage

    Reinforce non-sticky nourishment and the improved sensory experience.
