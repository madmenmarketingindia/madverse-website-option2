Dabur Glucose-D · Campaign Print

A nation invited.
Young athletes in motion.

Client
Dabur Glucose-D
Discipline
Print
Deliverables
Posters · Standees · T-shirts

Project overview

Grassroots potential.
Given a national starting line.

Ab Daudega Hindustan was created by Dabur Glucose-D to identify promising young track-and-field talent and connect deserving athletes with professional training.

Our print system carried that invitation into schools, colleges, academies and on-ground activations through posters, standees and T-shirts—giving the programme one energetic, recognisable voice.

Audience
Young Athletes
Purpose
Talent Discovery
Reach
Nationwide
Action
Register

The communication challenge

Inspire the dream.
Explain the first step.

01 · Ambition

The campaign needed the scale and optimism of a national sporting platform while staying relatable to young grassroots athletes.

02 · Participation

Every format had to move quickly from emotional invitation to practical registration information.

The human spark

One barefoot win.
Thousands of possible starts.

The initiative drew inspiration from young runner Sayali Mhaishunde, whose performance despite limited resources represented the unseen potential the platform hoped to discover.

The creative idea therefore focuses on movement before polish: footprints, a runner in full stride and the map of India transform one athlete’s first step into a national call.

The physical system

One rallying cry.
Three jobs to do.

  1. 01

    Posters

    Carry the purpose, registration path and participation details in one complete read.

  2. 02

    Standees

    Command attention at schools, academies, camps and activation venues.

  3. 03

    T-shirts

    Turn organisers and participants into moving expressions of the campaign identity.

The programme in motion

From outreach.
To opportunity.

8,000Entries received
350Athletes at the national camp
7Selected for free training

Official Dabur reporting records outreach across schools, colleges, sports academies and institutes, followed by screening and a national camp in Delhi.

The work in context

Energy from a distance.
Direction up close.

The campaign mark delivers immediate recognition; the detailed poster converts that attention into participation. Supplied visuals remain uncropped so the complete hierarchy is visible.

The outcome

A campaign people could see.
A pathway athletes could enter.

The physical system connected national ambition with clear local action, helping one identity travel consistently from awareness to registration and on-ground participation.
