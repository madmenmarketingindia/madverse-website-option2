Project overview

From product detail
to product desire.

A connected A+ content journey that helps shoppers understand the new Gulabari Face Toner Mist range at a glance.

The system moves from launch promise to ingredients, benefits, usage and range discovery—giving every frame one clear job while maintaining a soft, recognisable rose-led world.

The challenge

Make a new format
feel instantly
familiar.

01 · The task

Introduce two face-toner variants with immediate clarity around their ingredients, benefits and role in a daily skincare routine.

02 · The opportunity

Use Gulabari’s established rose equity to make a modern mist format feel credible, desirable and easy to understand.

The content strategy

Answer every question.
Keep the story
beautiful.

Let product education unfold as a simple visual sequence.

Each module resolves one shopper question—what it is, what is inside, what it does, how to use it and where it sits within the wider range.

  1. 01

    Lead with the promise

    Open with a clear Spray & Glow proposition and strong product visibility.

  2. 02

    Turn proof into pictures

    Make ingredients and benefits easy to scan through visual hierarchy.

  3. 03

    Close the journey

    Show usage and the wider range to support confident product choice.

The A+ showcase

One scroll.
A complete product story.

The content works as a guided sequence: benefit first, behaviour next and range discovery last.

The outcome

Easy to understand.
Hard to scroll past.

A modular commerce story brings product education and visual desire together without overwhelming the shopper.
