Fresh in flavour.
Ready for every meal.

Project overview

Give digestive care
a fresher point of view.

A connected A+ journey that turns Hajmola Pudina’s mint identity, traditional ingredients and digestive benefits into an easy, energetic product story.

The system moves from a fresh green product world into ingredient education, everyday occasions and colourful range discovery—always keeping the brand’s playful voice visible.

Brand
Hajmola
Variant
Pudina
Scope
A+ Commerce Content
Outputs
Product Story · Digital Assets

The challenge

Own the freshness.
Prove the
function.

01 · The task

Create a distinctive Pudina expression while clearly explaining the traditional ingredient blend behind its digestive role.

02 · The opportunity

Use mint greens and bold yellow copy to make a familiar digestive product feel active, social and contemporary.

The content strategy

Ground the story.
Then let it
move.

Start with recognisable ingredients, then take the product into life.

Cumin, black pepper, black salt and ginger establish purpose before energetic lifestyle frames show how naturally Hajmola belongs in everyday moments.

  1. 01

    Frame the ingredients

    Organise the traditional blend into clean, readable product cards.

  2. 02

    Translate into benefits

    Connect the blend to digestion, appetite and everyday wellbeing.

  3. 03

    Expand the occasions

    Move from home to office and social settings before revealing the full range.

The A+ showcase

Home. Work. Play.
One fresh habit.

Three occasion-led modules show Pudina moving easily through the day before opening into the complete Hajmola flavour family.

The outcome

Freshly distinctive.
Familiarly Hajmola.

The final system makes Pudina unmistakable while carrying forward the humour, usefulness and everyday accessibility of the wider brand.

01

Variant ownership

A strong green world makes Pudina instantly identifiable.

02

Functional clarity

Ingredients and benefits establish a useful reason to believe.

03

Broader relevance

Lifestyle moments position Hajmola as a small habit that travels anywhere.

Wherever the mood goes.Hajmola goes with it.
