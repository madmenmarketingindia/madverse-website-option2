Project overview

The day keeps moving.
Freshness moves with you.

Kara’s promise is built for life outside the dressing table: a quick refresh wherever the day happens. The film makes that portability the centre of an energetic travel story.

By placing the pack in a lively on-the-go moment, the communication turns convenience into confidence and keeps the product benefit immediate, visual and easy to remember.

The lifestyle film

One quick refresh.
Back into the experience.

The film gives product utility a lively context, showing how Kara fits naturally into travel, movement and spontaneous social moments.

The challenge

Make convenience desirable.
Make freshness feel instant.

01 · Show the moment

A wipe is a simple object. The communication needed a setting where its portable value becomes immediately meaningful.

02 · Keep product clarity

The pack, usage and refreshed expression had to remain readable inside a busy, energetic lifestyle environment.

The story rhythm

Move. Refresh.
Keep going.

  1. 01

    Set the pace

    Begin inside an active travel experience where freshness has real relevance.

  2. 02

    Show the solution

    Bring the pack and simple wipe action clearly into the centre of the moment.

  3. 03

    Return to life

    Resolve on renewed confidence and the freedom to stay present in the day.

The visual language

Clean product cues.
Colourful real life.

White wardrobe and bright packaging create freshness, while an open-top city-tour setting adds movement, sunlight and an unmistakable sense of life on the go.
