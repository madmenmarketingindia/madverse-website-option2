Xpert Dishwash · Brand Film

Less effort at the sink.
More care around it.

Client
Xpert
Discipline
Video Production
Role
Concept to Final Edit

Project overview

A cleaning benefit.
Told through shared responsibility.

A mother’s care is visible everywhere in a home. The effort behind it often is not.

We created and produced a film that reframes dishwashing around empathy: recognise the homemaker’s daily work, reduce her load and let Xpert make tough cleaning easier.

Format
Brand Film
Insight
Shared Effort
Product
Dishwash
Delivery
Production + Edit

The hero film

Make one less utensil.
Make one meaningful gesture.

A relatable household story transforms a familiar chore into a small, practical expression of care.

The challenge

Sell cleaning power.
Without losing human warmth.

01 · Familiar category

Dishwash films often rely on demonstration alone. Xpert needed an emotional reason to pay attention.

02 · Product resolution

The human story still had to land in a credible role for a brand built for burnt spices, turmeric marks and everyday cleaning.

The narrative approach

Observe the effort.
Invite a simple change.

  1. 01

    Recognise

    Begin with the constant, often unseen work of caring for a family.

  2. 02

    Reframe

    Turn fewer dirty utensils into an accessible act of participation.

  3. 03

    Resolve

    Let Xpert support the remaining clean-up with focused product confidence.

The visual storytelling

Real home texture.
Clear product payoff.

Warm performances and recognisable kitchen moments build empathy. Controlled product frames, cleaning action and the Xpert colour world deliver the functional close.

The outcome

A product film.
With a more human reason to care.

An end-to-end brand film that connects Xpert’s cleaning role to a culturally familiar household truth and a simple invitation to share the effort.
