Haldiram’s · Festive Social Campaign

Happy
Shappy Holi.

Client
Haldiram’s
Discipline
Campaigns
Year
2025

Project overview

Turn a festive ritual
into a memorable phrase.

Holi is colour, connection and the joy of feeding the people around you.

We gave that familiar behaviour a light, repeatable expression: “Haldiram’s mithai khaa, Happy Shappy ho jaa.” The campaign brings sweets and summer coolers into the celebration without interrupting its spontaneity.

Idea
Festive joy
Format
Social films
Product
Mithai + drinks
Energy
Colour-first

The challenge

Own the Holi moment
without feeling like an interruption.

01 · Cultural truth

Mithai is already part of the occasion. The brand needed to feel naturally present in celebration, not placed on top of it.

02 · Social behaviour

The idea had to land immediately in vertical video, survive the scroll and stay recognisable across multiple edits.

The strategy

One joyful hook.
Three social stories.

Build memory through repetition, then keep the content fresh through people, products and moments.

The line acts as the campaign’s chorus. Every reel finds a different way back to the same festive payoff.

  1. 01

    Ritual

    Start with recognisable Holi behaviour.

  2. 02

    Product

    Make mithai and coolers part of the action.

  3. 03

    Recall

    Close with one repeatable verbal signature.

The campaign world

Bright enough to stop.
Warm enough to share.

Vertical frames pair saturated Holi colour with bold, instantly readable language and clear product moments.

The impact

A festival phrase
made for participation.

A coherent social system that links celebration, product and recall without losing the warmth of the occasion.

01

Immediate recognition

Holi colour and behaviour communicate the moment before the copy is read.

02

Flexible storytelling

One platform supports multiple reel narratives and product combinations.

03

Brand-led joy

The product becomes part of sharing, not a pause in the celebration.
