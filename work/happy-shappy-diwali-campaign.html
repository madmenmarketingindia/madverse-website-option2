Project overview

Turn festive joy
into a creator-led movement.

Diwali is gifting, togetherness and the shared pleasure of mithai.

We expanded Happy Shappy into a multi-creator system spanning strategy, talent, concepts, shooting, production, edits, participation content and social amplification.

The challenge

Own the Diwali feed.
Never repeat the same story.

01 · Cultural truth

Mithai already belongs to Diwali. The campaign needed to turn that truth into distinctive, creator-native entertainment.

02 · Creative scale

Different influencers, formats and edits had to feel individual while building one recognisable Haldiram’s platform.

The strategy

One joyful platform.
Many creator expressions.

Build memory through one verbal platform, then create reach through different personalities and festive situations.

Dance, humour, food, family and reactions each offer a fresh route to the same Happy Shappy payoff.

  1. 01

    Find

    Match creator personality to a recognisable festive behaviour.

  2. 02

    Make

    Script, shoot and edit product naturally into the entertainment.

  3. 03

    Multiply

    Use consistent codes to compound recognition across the feed.
