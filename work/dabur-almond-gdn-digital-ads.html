Dabur Almond Shampoo · Amazon E-Banner

Simple enough to scan.
Strong enough to sell.

A marketplace-ready digital advertising system that gives the product, the promotional pairing and the next action a clear order.

Discipline

Project overview

Marketplace clarity.
Brand impact intact.

On Amazon, every visual must compete while working inside strict marketplace rules.

We created an e-banner built around one immediately understood pairing: Dabur Almond Shampoo and a promotional hair dryer. The design keeps the product dominant, the message compact and the action unmistakable.
