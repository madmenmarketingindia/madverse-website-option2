Built to buy.

Project overview

A broad catalogue.
A composed experience.

Home products need room to be considered, compared and imagined in context.

We created a responsive commerce concept that gives categories a clear hierarchy, lets imagery lead and keeps product discovery connected to purchase.

Platform
Online Store
Focus
Home Products

Responsive Web

The organising idea

Let products breathe.
Keep shopping moving.

The visual system uses generous white space, disciplined navy framing and large lifestyle imagery to give every product category a premium presence.

Strong navigation and direct shopping actions keep that editorial quality useful—not decorative.

The commerce challenge

Show range without clutter.
Create desire without delay.

01 · Catalogue clarity

Multiple rooms, categories and products must remain easy to understand and quick to navigate.

02 · Visual confidence

Product imagery must feel aspirational while prices, names and purchase actions stay immediately usable.

The shopping journey

From room inspiration.
To the right product.

  1. 01

    Arrive

    A strong category feature establishes the store and current collection.

  2. 02

    Browse

    Room and product categories provide immediate routes into the catalogue.

  3. 03

    Compare

    Consistent cards make names, imagery and prices easy to scan.

  4. 04

    Choose

    Focused product actions shorten the path from discovery to purchase.

The complete experience

Editorial in feel.
Commercial in function.

The homepage moves from lifestyle-led category discovery into a structured product catalogue, while the secondary experience gives individual ranges room to develop.

The commerce system

Quiet interface.
Clear commercial purpose.

01

Category first

Room and product groupings turn a large assortment into clear entry points.

02

Image led

Lifestyle and product photography carry the desirability of each collection.

03

Consistent cards

A repeatable product language improves scanning and comparison.

04

Responsive flow

Hierarchy and shopping actions remain clear from large screens to phones.

Responsive by design

The catalogue adapts.
The hierarchy stays.

Desktop gives the product world room to unfold. Tablet and mobile compress the same system into focused, touch-friendly browsing without losing category context.

The outcome

A storefront with space.
A journey with direction.

A responsive product experience that balances inspiration and commerce—helping shoppers understand the range, find relevant products and move naturally toward purchase.

01

02

Stronger desire
