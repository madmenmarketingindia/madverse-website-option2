Project overview

A functional paint.
Given visible appeal.

Acroshield is a water-thinnable exterior emulsion designed to combine surface protection with a clean decorative finish.

Our print direction brought the product, painted environment and pack identity together in one immediate frame—making performance tangible for homeowners while keeping the communication useful for professional painters.

Category
Exterior Emulsion
Audience
Homeowners + Painters
Role

System

The communication challenge

Sell the protection.
Show the finish.

01 · Product clarity

The communication needed to explain an exterior-protection product without depending on technical language alone.

02 · Market distinction

The pack and print system had to remain recognisable in a crowded paint category across different applications.

The organising idea

Show the wall.
Anchor the product.

A finished room makes colour and coverage visible before copy is read. The pack then anchors the promise in a real, purchasable product.

This simple relationship turns an abstract coating benefit into a scene people can imagine in their own space.
