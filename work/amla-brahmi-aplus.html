Project overview

Turn a familiar ritual
into a richer product story.

A connected A+ journey that makes Dabur Brahmi Amla Hair Oil easy to understand, trust and choose on the digital shelf.

The system translates a well-known hair-oiling ritual into a modern commerce experience—moving from product promise to ingredients, benefits, usage and the wider Dabur legacy.

Brand
Dabur
Product
Brahmi Amla Hair Oil
Scope
A+ Commerce Content
Outputs
Product Story · Digital Assets

The challenge

Make heritage feel
clear, current
and compelling.

01 · The task

Communicate the product’s ingredient story and hair benefits quickly, without losing the warmth and authority of a trusted household brand.

02 · The opportunity

Build one lush visual world where nature, product efficacy and everyday hair care feel naturally connected.

The content strategy

Start with nature.
Build towards
proof.

Let every frame answer one practical shopper question.

The sequence begins with what is inside, translates ingredients into visible benefits, demonstrates the ritual and closes with the confidence of Dabur’s hair-oil portfolio.

  1. 01

    Make ingredients tangible

    Bring Brahmi Amla extract, almond oil and Vitamin E into one instantly readable composition.

  2. 02

    Connect claim to outcome

    Translate the formula into growth, shine, strength and improved texture.

  3. 03

    Complete the ritual

    Make usage simple, then anchor the product within Dabur’s long-standing hair-care legacy.

The A+ showcase

From benefit
to ritual to legacy.

A three-part commerce sequence carries the shopper from desired hair outcomes to confident everyday use.

The outcome

Deeply rooted.
Digitally ready.

A cohesive product story gives shoppers the clarity of commerce content without losing the richness of the brand world.
