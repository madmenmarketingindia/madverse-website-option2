Sound made visible.
Identity turned up.

Client
DGK

Artwork + Content

Project overview

An album identity.
Built from the sound outward.

DGK needed cover artwork and content that could introduce the release before a listener heard the first beat.

We translated the performer-producer persona into a dark, layered visual system—combining live-performance energy, studio symbolism and a compact typographic voice.

The creative challenge

Capture the performance.
Reveal the producer.

01 · Dual identity

The artwork had to hold the energy of a live performer and the precision of a studio producer in one image.

02 · Small-format impact

Layered detail needed to remain distinctive when reduced to the compact scale of an album cover.

The visual direction

Dark room.
Live signal.

Black creates the stage. Deep red behaves like a recording light. Fragments of microphone, performer and production graphics make the cover feel caught between a live set and a studio session.

The result is deliberately dense but controlled: an image with enough atmosphere to reward a second look and enough hierarchy to read immediately.

The cover system

Image. Signal.
Role.

01

Performance

A cropped figure and microphone put human energy at the centre.

02

Production

Audio symbols and technical overlays reveal the craft behind the sound.

03

Content

Performer, producer, sound concept designer and rapper form a compact identity statement.

The work in context

Album-scale artwork.
Street-scale presence.

The environmental mockups test the cover’s contrast and recognition beyond its native format. Both supplied visuals remain uncropped so the complete composition stays visible.

The outcome

A cover with rhythm.
A persona with range.

The final artwork brings performance, production and authorship into one recognisable frame—giving the release a visual world before playback begins.
