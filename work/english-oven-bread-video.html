Shuruaat
Great.

Client
English Oven
Discipline
Video
Year
2022

Project overview

An everyday breakfast.
A better beginning.

Morning is a small window where food, family and possibility meet.

The campaign turns a familiar loaf into the start of something positive. Warm family storytelling, appetising food moments and a direct verbal hook make English Oven part of the everyday breakfast ritual.

Format
Brand film
Story
Family morning
Product
Bread range
Platform
Film + OOH

The hero film

Karo har din ki
shuruaat great.

A simple, optimistic film that makes product presence feel natural inside the rhythm of a real household.

The challenge

Make packaged bread
feel freshly personal.

01 · Familiar category

Bread is habitual and highly functional. The communication needed to add warmth and preference without overcomplicating the product truth.

02 · Family relevance

The story had to work across households and age groups while keeping the brand and range clearly visible.

The creative approach

Start with emotion.
Land on appetite.

Build the film around a morning people recognise, then let the product make that moment feel more complete.

The line gives every execution a consistent ending while family casts and product variants keep the system flexible.

  1. 01

    Recognise

    A warm, believable household morning.

  2. 02

    Desire

    Food and pack moments with appetite appeal.

  3. 03

    Remember

    One optimistic phrase built for repetition.

The visual system

A warm world
built around breakfast.

The outcome

One morning thought.
A complete campaign world.

The film establishes the emotion; the product and outdoor adaptations extend it into a recognisable brand system.

01

Human relevance

A category staple becomes part of a positive family ritual.

02

Clear product role

White, milk and brown bread variants fit naturally into the same promise.

03

Media flexibility

The central line travels cleanly across film, digital frames and outdoor formats.

Everyday food.A great beginning.
