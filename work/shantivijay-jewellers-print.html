Shantivijay Jewellers · Business Cards

Quiet luxury.
Made personal.

Client
Shantivijay Jewellers
Discipline
Print
Deliverable
Business Cards

Project overview

A small format.
A lasting impression.

For a jeweller with roots reaching back to 1973, every introduction needed to carry the same confidence as the craft itself.

We designed a business-card identity that feels refined rather than decorative—balancing a generous field of white, precise typography and a restrained gold seahorse mark.

Established
1973
Category
Fine Jewellery
Expression
Elegant + Restrained
Signature
Gold Seahorse

The design challenge

Signal prestige.
Never overstate it.

01 · Character

The card needed to express jewellery-grade attention to detail without relying on visual excess.

02 · Function

Contact information had to remain effortless to read while the identity retained a composed, premium presence.

The creative principle

Elegance lives
in restraint.

The design borrows its confidence from fine jewellery: one distinctive symbol, careful proportion and enough space for every detail to breathe.

Instead of adding ornament, the system uses hierarchy and material contrast to make the name, the individual and the contact information feel considered.

The identity anatomy

Three details.
One refined signature.

  1. 01

    The mark

    A gold seahorse creates a memorable focal point with the delicacy of a crafted ornament.

  2. 02

    The name

    Measured letter spacing gives the wordmark authority without making it feel heavy.

  3. 03

    The space

    A calm white field lets the identity and essential information remain the only priorities.

The work in context

Personal by scale.
Recognisable anywhere.

The supplied mockups enlarge the business-card identity to reveal its hierarchy and finish. Both compositions are shown uncropped, preserving the complete artwork.

The outcome

An introduction.
With the weight of a signature.

The final direction distilled the brand into a tactile, composed first impression—premium enough for the category and practical enough for every personal exchange.

01

Instant recognition

The singular gold mark makes the identity easy to remember.

02

Premium restraint

White space and measured typography communicate quiet confidence.

03

Useful hierarchy

Name, brand and contact details remain clear at first glance.

Less decoration.More distinction.
