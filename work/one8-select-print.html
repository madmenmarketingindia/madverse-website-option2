Project overview

Carry the attitude.
Down to the last detail.

The One8 Select print system translated a confident formal-footwear identity into packaging, retail display and outdoor communication.

Every element—from the campaign portrait and headline to boxes, bags and care products—needed to feel polished, contemporary and recognisably part of one premium brand.

The design language

Black for confidence.
Gold for distinction.

A restrained black-and-gold palette gives the identity an immediate premium register. Warm neutrals and wood surfaces keep it tactile rather than overly formal.

The campaign line connects aspiration to footwear, while Virat Kohli gives the system a recognisable expression of modern confidence.
