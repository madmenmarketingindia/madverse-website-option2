Frequently asked questions

Before we
get building.

A few practical answers to help you plan your website. We will confirm the details around your project brief.

Can you redesign our existing website?

Yes. We start by reviewing your current website, content and key journeys. Together, we identify what to retain, what to improve and whether the existing platform supports your goals.

How much will the website cost?

The cost depends on the number of page types, design requirements, content, integrations and features. After discussing your brief, we propose a defined scope and quote so you can see what is included.

How long does a website project take?

We agree a timeline after understanding the scope. Content readiness, integrations and review milestones all affect the schedule. Your project plan will identify where we need input from your team.

Do we need to supply the content?

You can provide existing copy, imagery and brand guidelines. If you need help creating or organising content, we can include that in the scope. We agree responsibilities early so content is ready for the build.

Will the website work on mobile and support SEO?

Responsive layouts and essential on-page SEO are part of the approach. We plan headings, page titles and content structure alongside design, then check the experience across screen sizes. Ongoing search optimisation can be scoped separately.

Can our team update the website after launch?

If your team needs to edit content, we plan an appropriate content-management setup as part of the brief. The handover covers the agreed editing tools and how to manage routine updates.

Can you connect forms, analytics and business tools?

We can scope enquiry forms, analytics and integrations with your existing tools. We first review the available access and technical requirements, then agree what will be connected and tested.

What support is available after launch?

Maintenance, updates and ongoing improvements can be included in a separate support arrangement. We agree responsibilities, coverage and next steps before handover so your team knows where to get help.
