Our process

From the brief
to the big moment.

Clear decisions keep the campaign moving. At every stage, we agree the direction with you before taking the idea further.

  1. 01

    Brief

    Understand the business goal, audience, timing and budget. Agree what the campaign needs to achieve.

    Align on the challenge
  2. 02

    Strategy

    Define the audience insight, central message and role of each channel so the creative has a clear purpose.

    Set the direction
  3. 03

    Concept

    Explore creative routes, key visuals and messaging. Develop the chosen idea with your feedback.

    Approve the idea
  4. 04

    Production

    Create the agreed films, imagery, copy and channel assets, with review points to keep everything connected.

    Review the campaign assets
  5. 05

    Launch & learn

    Prepare approved assets for release, coordinate the agreed rollout and review available results to inform the next move.

    Take the idea into the world

Timelines, production requirements and launch responsibilities are agreed around your brief, channels and review schedule.

Frequently asked questions

Before the brief.
A little clarity.

Planning a launch or exploring an idea? These answers explain how we shape a campaign around your needs.

What do you need from us to get started?

Tell us about your brand, audience, campaign goal, timing and budget range. Existing brand guidelines and previous work are useful too. If the brief is still taking shape, we can help identify the questions to answer first.

Can you help with both strategy and execution?

Yes. We can scope the work from campaign strategy and concepts through production and channel adaptations. We agree responsibilities and deliverables before the project begins.

How is a campaign budget decided?

The budget depends on the creative scope, channels, production requirements, talent and number of assets. We prepare a proposal around the brief and clarify any separate costs, such as media spend or licensing.

How long does a campaign take?

Timing depends on the idea, production needs and approval schedule. Share your intended launch date early so we can plan the scope, review milestones and delivery sequence around it.

Can you work with our existing brand or campaign idea?

Yes. We can develop an existing direction, adapt it for new channels or create a new campaign within your brand guidelines. We review the available materials and usage rights as part of the brief.

Are films, photography and creator content included?

These can be included where the campaign needs them. Production, casting, creator involvement, edit versions and usage terms are agreed in the scope, so you know what the proposed budget covers.

What will we receive at the end?

You receive the approved campaign assets in the agreed formats. The proposal defines deliverables, versions, revisions and handover requirements, including any editable files and licensing arrangements.

Do you handle media and campaign reporting?

Media planning, buying and reporting can be discussed alongside the creative work and scoped separately where needed. We agree who manages launch activity, which measures matter and what data will be available for review.
