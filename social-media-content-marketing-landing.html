Frequently asked questions

Before we post.
Let's get clear.

A few practical answers to help you plan your social support. Channels, deliverables and working arrangements are agreed around your brief.

What do you need to get started?

Share your brand goals, audience, existing social channels, current challenges and an indicative budget. Brand guidelines, past content and available performance reports help us understand the starting point. Any account access needed is agreed during onboarding.

Which social channels should we focus on?

We recommend a channel mix around your audience, objectives and capacity to create useful content. You do not need to be everywhere: the plan should prioritise the places where your brand has a clear role.

How many posts and reels are included?

Content volumes, formats and publishing frequency are defined in the scope. We balance your priorities and budget across static creative, carousels, stories and video rather than assuming the same mix suits every brand.

Is video production included?

Reel concepts, editing and shoot requirements are agreed as part of the content plan. Dedicated shoots, locations, talent and other production needs should be scoped explicitly; they are not automatically included in every social engagement.

Will we approve content before it goes live?

Yes. We agree a review schedule and approval route for the calendar, creative and captions. Consolidated feedback helps keep publishing on track. Time-sensitive content also follows an agreed approval process.

Do you manage comments and direct messages?

Community management can be included within agreed channels and coverage hours. Response guidelines and escalation contacts are set with your team so questions needing customer service or specialist input reach the right person. Round-the-clock coverage is not assumed.

How do you report on progress?

We agree relevant measures and a reporting cadence upfront. Depending on your objectives and available data, reviews can cover reach, engagement, video viewing and website actions, with recommendations for the next content cycle. Follower growth or sales are not guaranteed.

Are paid ads and creator partnerships included?

Paid media, advertising spend and creator partnerships are scoped separately unless explicitly included in the proposal. Share these requirements early so organic content and campaign activity can be planned together.
