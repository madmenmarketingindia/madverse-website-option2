About the show

Different
voices
Shared
curiosity

Every brand has a story.
The people behind it have more.

Mad Talks is the voice of the Madverse. A space for founders, creators, marketers and culture-shapers to talk about the choices, challenges and ideas behind their work.

We bring different perspectives into the same conversation, connecting creativity, business and culture through a shared curiosity about what makes growth happen.

A few good questions

Before you
tune in

A little more about the conversations, the people and how to get involved.

Who is Mad Talks for?

Founders, creators, marketers and anyone curious about how brands, businesses and culture move forward. You don’t need to work in the industry to bring a fresh perspective.

What kinds of conversations can I expect?

The choices behind the work, the lessons along the way and the questions shaping what comes next. Mad Talks connects perspectives on creativity, leadership, business, culture and growth.

Can I suggest myself or someone else as a guest?

Yes. Tell us who you have in mind, what they do and the perspective they could bring. You can suggest a guest through the invitation section above.

Where can I explore more Madverse ideas?

Visit Think for written perspectives on growth, creativity, leadership and culture, or explore our work to see ideas put into practice.
