How we work

Find the character.
Shape the identity.

From the first conversation to the final artwork, each stage brings your brand into clearer focus.

  1. Discover

    Understand your brand, audience and category. Review what exists and identify what the new identity needs to communicate.

  2. Define

    Agree the creative direction, visual references and design priorities, giving every decision a shared foundation.

  3. Design

    Develop the identity and key applications. Refine the chosen direction through feedback, checking consistency across formats and pack variants.

Frequently asked questions

Before we design.
Let's get clear.

A few practical answers to help you plan your identity or packaging project.

What do you need to get started?

Share your brand story, audience, goals and existing assets. For packaging, include product details, pack dimensions and any printer-supplied dielines available.

Can you refresh an existing brand?

Yes. We review what should stay recognisable and what needs to evolve, then agree whether the brief calls for a focused refresh or a broader redesign.

What is included in the project?

The proposal defines the identity assets, packaging variants, guidelines and rollout materials included. Naming, copywriting and additional applications are discussed separately where needed.

How long does the design process take?

Timing depends on the scope, number of pack variants and review stages. We agree milestones upfront, with time for feedback, approvals and final artwork preparation.

How do concepts and revisions work?

Concept routes and revision rounds are agreed in the scope. Consolidated feedback helps refine the chosen direction; additional routes or changes to an approved brief may affect cost and timing.

Which files will we receive?

We agree a handover list covering approved artwork, logo formats, guidelines and editable files where included. Any third-party font or image licensing requirements are identified separately.

Do you handle printing and production?

Printing and physical production are scoped separately. Final artwork is prepared to agreed specifications, with printer proofs and production sign-off responsibilities confirmed before release.

Who supplies and approves packaging information?

Your team supplies and approves product copy, claims and required pack information. We organise it within the design; specialist compliance review is not assumed to be included.
