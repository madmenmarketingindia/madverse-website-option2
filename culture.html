The principles
behind the people.

Not posters on a wall. These are the behaviors we expect from ourselves and protect as we grow.

  1. 01

    Stay curious

    Ask better questions, look beyond the familiar and keep learning from every discipline.

  2. 02

    Move together

    Share context early, invite different perspectives and make the work stronger as one team.

  3. 03

    Own the outcome

    Take responsibility beyond the task and care about the impact the work creates.

  4. 04

    Keep it human

    Be direct, generous and respectful—especially when the pressure or ambition is high.
