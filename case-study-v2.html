Project overview

Refreshment with
a playful point
of view.

Thanda Funda turns a simple product moment into a bright, energetic campaign world built around flavour, freshness and visual appetite.

The system places the product at the centre of every frame, using bold colour, expressive movement and fruit-led imagery to create a distinctive, flexible look across campaign formats.

The challenge

Make every flavour
feel unmistakably
Thanda Funda.

01 · The task

Create a campaign system that could introduce a varied flavoured dairy range with immediate product clarity and strong shelf-to-screen recognition.

02 · The opportunity

Turn the range itself into the story—giving every flavour a distinctive world while keeping the family visually connected.

The strategy

One simple idea.
Many flavourful
expressions.

Build recognition through repetition, then create freshness through variation.

The product, name and bold tonal world stay constant. Colour, ingredients and situational storytelling shift to give each flavour its own reason to be noticed.

  1. 01

    Product first

    Keep the bottle large, clear and central so every execution works at a glance.

  2. 02

    Flavour as a world

    Use distinctive colour and ingredient cues to make each variant instantly recognisable.

  3. 03

    Flexible consistency

    Hold onto one strong visual structure while adapting the story across formats and moments.

The creative solution

A campaign world
full of flavour.

The visual language combines product-scale confidence with playful storytelling—moving from ingredient-rich product worlds to energetic everyday moments.

The impact

Built to stay clear.
Designed to keep
moving.

Across the campaign system, three practical strengths help the idea remain clear, recognisable and ready to expand.

01

Immediate range clarity

Strong flavour coding helps audiences distinguish variants while recognising one connected family.

02

Consistent product focus

The product remains central and readable across ingredient-led and lifestyle-led executions.

03

Room to expand

A repeatable structure makes it possible to introduce new flavours, messages and moments without losing recognition.

One recognisable idea.
Multiple ways to make it fresh.
