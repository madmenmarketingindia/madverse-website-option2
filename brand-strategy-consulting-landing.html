Frequently asked questions

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Before the first workshop, a little clarity on how we work, what shapes the scope and where the strategy can take you.

What do you need from us to get started?

Start with your business goals, the brand challenge and any existing research or brand materials. We use an initial conversation to understand the decisions ahead and shape the brief.

Is this for new brands or established businesses?

Both. A new brand may need its first position and story. An established business may need to sharpen its difference, enter a new market or bring its brand in line with how the business has evolved.

How long does a brand strategy project take?

Timing depends on the research required, the number of audiences or markets and stakeholder availability. We agree a schedule with clear review points once the scope is defined.

How is the project priced?

The proposal reflects the research, workshops and deliverables your brief needs. We agree the scope and fees before starting, with any additional design, content or campaign work scoped separately.

Will you work with our existing research and brand?

Yes. We review what you already have, identify what still holds true and look for gaps. That helps us decide where fresh research or a change in direction would be useful.

How involved will our team need to be?

Your team brings the business context. We agree who needs to contribute to discovery, workshops and key reviews, so the people responsible for using the strategy help shape it.

Does strategy include a new logo or campaign?

A strategy engagement defines the direction and the agreed strategic tools. Identity design, content production and campaign execution can follow through the collective, with their own agreed scope.

Can our in-house team put the strategy into practice?

Yes. The agreed framework, messaging guidance and roadmap give your team a reference for briefs and decisions. We walk through the direction together and can discuss further support where needed.
