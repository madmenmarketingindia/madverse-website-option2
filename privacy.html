How we collect, use and protect personal information when you interact with Madverse.

01

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Madverse (“Madverse”, “we”, “us” or “our”) handles personal information when you visit this website, contact us, ask about our services, apply to work with us or request communications from us.

We process personal information only for clear and lawful purposes and aim to collect only what is reasonably necessary for those purposes.

02

Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Contact details such as your name, email address, telephone number, company and role.
  • Information you include in enquiries, project briefs, applications, portfolios or other messages.
  • Your email address and communication preferences when you request the Madverse Brief or similar updates.
  • Basic technical information that our hosting or security providers may record, such as IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, requested pages and timestamps.

Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is genuinely necessary and we have asked you to provide it.

03

How we use information

We may use personal information to respond to enquiries, evaluate potential projects or applications, deliver requested communications, operate and secure the website, maintain business records, comply with legal obligations and protect our legitimate rights.

Where consent is the appropriate basis, you may withdraw it by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

04

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with companies that support our hosting, email, security, professional advice or business operations, only where needed for the relevant service and subject to appropriate safeguards.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to respond to lawful requests, or to protect Madverse, our users or others from harm. If a service provider processes information outside India, we will apply safeguards required by applicable law.

05

Retention and security

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including legal, accounting, dispute-resolution and security requirements. Retention depends on the nature of the interaction and applicable obligations.

We use reasonable organisational and technical measures designed to protect information. No online service or transmission method can, however, be guaranteed completely secure.

06

Cookies and website data

The current website does not include advertising trackers or analytics scripts. Essential storage or technical logs may still be used by the browser, hosting provider or security infrastructure to deliver and protect the website. If we later add non-essential analytics or advertising technologies, we will update this policy and provide any controls required by law.

07

Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, you may ask for information about the personal data we process, request correction or erasure, withdraw consent, nominate another person where the law permits, or raise a grievance about our handling of your information.

We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. You can also stop marketing emails at any time by using an unsubscribe option where provided or by contacting us.

08

Children’s privacy

This website is intended for a general business audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and address it.

09

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, technology or legal obligations change. The latest version will appear on this page with an updated date. Material changes may also be communicated through another appropriate channel.

10

Contact and grievances

For privacy questions, requests or grievances, contact Madverse at team@madmen.in, call +91 11 4562 2687, or write to F-301, 2nd Floor, CH. Prem Singh Comp., Lado Sarai, New Delhi, Delhi 110030. Please use the subject line “Privacy Request” and explain what you need so we can respond appropriately.

This policy should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before launch and confirmed against Madverse’s registered entity details and actual service-provider arrangements.
